An offline document scanner and PDF maker


Concept: Turn photographs of documents into clean, readable PDFs directly on the phone.

Core features
Capture documents using the camera.
Automatically detect document edges.
Crop and straighten pages.
Apply grayscale and contrast filters.
Combine multiple pages into one PDF.
Rename and organize scanned documents.
Save PDFs locally.
Share or export finished documents.
How it works offline

Document capture, image processing, page ordering, and PDF generation can all happen on the device.

How you could differentiate it

Focus on a lightweight scanner that doesn't require an account, upload documents to a server, or place essential functionality behind a subscription.

Potential audience: Students, teachers, office workers, small businesses, and people who frequently submit documents.

Main challenge: Image processing, camera quality, and document detection require more engineering than a basic notes app.

You should also avoid claiming that scanned documents are automatically legally certified or equivalent to official originals.
